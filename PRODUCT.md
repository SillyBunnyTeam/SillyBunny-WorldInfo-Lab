# Product

## Register

product

## Users

SillyBunny users who create, maintain, or troubleshoot lorebooks. They use World Info Lab while reviewing an active chat, reproducing activation behavior, checking saved cases, or applying deliberate bulk edits.

## Product Purpose

World Info Lab explains which lorebook entries SillyBunny would activate and why, without sending a message or silently changing lorebook state. It helps users diagnose matching behavior, preserve repeatable checks, understand prompt-space use, and review batch changes before saving them.

## Brand Personality

Precise, calm, and trustworthy. The interface should explain complex behavior plainly, distinguish simulation from mutation, and make consequential actions feel deliberate rather than alarming.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative dashboards, modal-heavy workflows, unexplained automation, cramped desktop-only layouts, and interfaces that obscure whether an action reads, simulates, or writes data.

## Design Principles

- Put the troubleshooting task ahead of extension chrome.
- Follow SillyBunny's native interaction patterns instead of inventing parallel navigation.
- Explain outcomes and failure states with specific, actionable language.
- Keep simulations safe by default and require explicit review before mutation.
- Preserve useful context across a troubleshooting session without retaining private content unnecessarily.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support keyboard and screen-reader navigation, visible focus, semantic status updates, reduced motion, coarse-pointer touch targets, responsive mobile layouts, and state cues that do not rely on color alone. Aim for WCAG AA contrast and interaction behavior.
